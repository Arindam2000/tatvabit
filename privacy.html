Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

Tatvabit is a small video channel. This policy covers the Tatvabit uploader, a private tool that publishes Tatvabit's own videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (Google API Services): it uploads videos, captions and thumbnails to the Tatvabit channel, sets their schedule, and reads that channel's own analytics (views, watch time, audience retention).
  • Instagram (Meta Graph API): it publishes Reels to the Tatvabit Instagram account and reads that account's own post insights.

The tool is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own accounts. It does not access any other person's account or data.

What we store

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Analytics numbers are kept locally as plain notes to improve future videos. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to any third party.

Tatvabit's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own terms apply: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers

If you watch or comment on Tatvabit videos, YouTube and Instagram handle your data under their own policies. Tatvabit sees only the aggregate numbers and public comments those platforms show every channel.

Removing access and data deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in Google account permissions or in Instagram / Facebook settings under Apps and Websites. Revoking access stops the tool, and the owner deletes the local tokens and notes. If you believe Tatvabit holds any data about you, send a message to @tatvabit on Instagram and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Changes

Any change to this policy is posted on this page with a new date.
